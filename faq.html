Frequently asked questions

Is this legal advice?

No. This site is educational only. For advice about your case, talk to a licensed immigration attorney or a DOJ-accredited representative.

Are these the official interview questions?

Only the 128 civics questions in the naturalization guide come directly from USCIS. Everything labeled 'commonly reported practice questions' is general preparation and not an official list.

Are the guides really free?

Yes. All PDF guides on this site are free to download, with no account required.

Where do I check my case status?

Use the official USCIS case status tools at uscis.gov or my.uscis.gov.

How do I find affordable legal help?

USCIS keeps a page on finding legal services, including DOJ-accredited representatives at recognized nonprofits.

How do I avoid immigration scams?

Never pay anyone who promises guaranteed results. USCIS forms are free to download from uscis.gov. Report suspected fraud at uscis.gov/report-fraud.

What is PathwayUS?

PathwayUS is a tool you can use to organize your documents and track your progress. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.
