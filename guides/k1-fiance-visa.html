K-1 Fiancé(e) Visa Consular Interview

After USCIS approves Form I-129F, the foreign fiancé(e) applies for a K-1 visa and is interviewed by a consular officer at a U.S. embassy or consulate.

Download the free PDF guide (47 KB)

Key facts from official sources

  • A U.S. citizen files Form I-129F with USCIS to bring a foreign fiancé(e) to the United States; after approval, the fiancé(e) applies for the K-1 visa abroad. [source 1]
  • The couple must marry within 90 days of the fiancé(e)'s admission to the United States; afterward the spouse may apply for permanent residence (Form I-485). [source 1]

Commonly reported practice questions

Commonly reported practice questions: these are general topics people report being asked about. They are NOT an official USCIS or Department of State list. Officers may ask anything related to your case. Answer truthfully and consistently with your forms.

  • How you met and how often you have seen each other in person.
  • When and how the proposal happened.
  • Your wedding plans after arrival.
  • Your fiancé(e)'s job, family and where you will live.
  • Questions confirming information on the petition and visa application.

Document checklist

Follow your interview notice or embassy instructions first.

  • Valid passport
  • Interview appointment letter and DS-160 confirmation (per embassy instructions)
  • Medical exam results (from an embassy-approved physician)
  • Police certificates and civil documents as listed by the embassy
  • Evidence of your relationship: photos together, travel records, communication history
  • Financial support documents as requested (e.g., Form I-134)

Interview day tips

  • Follow the specific embassy's instructions; requirements vary by post.
  • Bring originals plus copies.
  • Answer simply and truthfully; consistency with the petition matters.
